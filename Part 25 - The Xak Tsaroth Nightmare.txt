IMMEDIATE DEPARTURE TO HAVEN
"We're leaving. Now." Johannes's voice is firm. "Mission accomplished - the 
Dragon Army operation is destroyed. What happens next with that thing is 
beyond our capability. We report to Laurana and let people with ancient lore 
and divine authority handle re-containment."
Cyrus nods with visible relief. "Agreed. That entity is stable for now, but 
we're not equipped to deal with primordial forces. The resistance needs to 
know, and quickly."
Kessa takes one last look at the glowing excavation site. "At least the 
Dragon Army got exactly what they deserved for digging where they shouldn't. 
Let's go before that thing decides it's hungry again."
 
DEPARTURE - NIGHT OF DAY 6
The party moves away from Xak Tsaroth under cover of darkness, putting 
distance between themselves and the awakened entity. The blue-green glow is 
visible for miles behind you, a pillar marking the ruins.
The journey back is uneventful but tense. The nightmares that plagued 
Cyrus and Kessa fade with distance - by the second night, they sleep peacefully 
again.
 
DAY 11 - RETURN TO HAVEN
You arrive at Haven's gates travel-worn but alive. Word of your return spreads 
quickly, and within the hour, you're in Commander Laurana's command tent.
DEBRIEFING - COMMANDER LAURANA
She listens intently as Johannes reports:
*	Dragon Army excavation at Xak Tsaroth
*	Pre-Cataclysm imprisonment of primordial fragment
*	Pump sabotage and flooding
*	Accidental release of the entity
*	Dragon Army casualties and retreat
*	Current status: Entity dormant at excavation site
When Johannes finishes, Laurana is silent for a long moment.
"You destroyed a 60-soldier Dragon Army operation with zero 
friendly casualties. That's extraordinary."
"You also inadvertently released something that ancient priests 
couldn't destroy and could only imprison. That's... concerning."
She studies the map. "But realistically, you had no good options. If the Dragon 
Army had breached those seals on their terms, they might have found a way to 
control or weaponize it. Instead, they're scattered, traumatized, and the entity 
remains at the excavation site not actively threatening anyone."
"That's a tactical win even if it's not a clean one."
 
STRATEGIC ASSESSMENT
Laurana marks Xak Tsaroth on her map with a red warning symbol.
"We'll establish a perimeter - keep civilians and soldiers away. I'll send word 
to Goldmoon and Elistan. If anyone can research how to re-seal a primordial 
fragment, it's our high clerics working with the Disks."
She looks at you three. "This may require another divine intervention. Another 
Dargaard Keep situation where we bring holy authority to bear on something 
beyond mortal capability."
"But that's future work. For now: you've neutralized a Dragon Army threat, 
prevented them from gaining a weapon, and returned with critical 
intelligence."
 
REWARDS & RECOGNITION
"First, compensation for services rendered:"
*	3,000 gold pieces (1,000 gp each) - standard mission payment
*	Commendations - formal recognition within resistance hierarchy
*	Priority requisitions - access to resistance armory/supplies as 
needed
"Second, I have something special for you, Brother Cyrus."
Laurana retrieves a wooden case and opens it, revealing a beautifully 
crafted holy symbol of Mishakal - platinum and sapphires, clearly ancient 
and valuable.
"This was recovered from a pre-Cataclysm temple. Our clerics examined it and 
confirmed it's a Periapt of Wound Closure - a rare healing artifact. You've 
proven yourself worthy of such a relic."
PERIAPT OF WOUND CLOSURE (Rare, Attunement)
*	Stabilize automatically when dying (don't need to make death saves)
*	Regain maximum hit points from Hit Dice spent during short rest
"Wear it with honor, Brother Cyrus. Mishakal has clearly marked you as one of 
her champions."
Cyrus accepts with genuine reverence, tears in his eyes. "I will honor 
this gift. Thank you, Commander."
 
LAURANA CONTINUES
"You three have become something remarkable. Dargaard Keep. Xak Tsaroth. 
Pax Tharkas before that. You handle threats others can't even comprehend."
"The resistance is winning, slowly, because of operatives like you. The Dragon 
Army is powerful, but you've cost them fortress, fleets, excavations, and 
countless soldiers."
She looks at each of you seriously. "Rest. Recover. Spend your gold. You've 
earned it. When I need you again - and I will - I'll send word."
"Dismissed. And thank you. Truly."
