OOC - Excellent question, and it's actually a matter of significant debate in D&D lore!

RAW (Rules As Written) in 5e: The PHB is deliberately vague. It says warlocks gain power 
through a pact, but doesn't explicitly state what happens if the pact breaks.

Common Interpretations:

"Knowledge Once Gained" interpretation: You've already learned how to channel eldritch power 
through your patron's teachings. Breaking the pact doesn't unlearn that knowledge. You keep 
your powers like a wizard keeps their spellbook knowledge.
"Ongoing Connection" interpretation: Your powers require active connection to the patron. 
Break the pact = lose the powers (harsh, but thematically appropriate for some patrons).
"Middle Ground" interpretation: You keep existing abilities but can't advance further as a warlock 
without a new patron.
